i tube Privacy Policy

i tube runs the Instagram account @yourstoryyourshow. You send a real-life story by direct message, we turn it into a comic-style video, and after you approve it we publish it on our account.

What we collect

Your Instagram user ID, the messages you send us, and the drafts, drawings and videos made from them. We do not ask for photos of you and we do not use real photos in videos.

How we use it

Only to reply to you, write your story and make and publish its video. A published video is public on Instagram. We do not sell your data or use it for advertising.

Who processes it

Your messages are sent to OpenAI and TypeSafe, which write the story and check it for safety. Meta delivers the messages and hosts published videos. Drawings and narration are made on our own computers.

How long we keep it

On our own servers, until you ask us to delete it.

Deleting your data

Email mehmet.ulutug@gmail.com with your Instagram username. Within 30 days we delete your messages, drafts and media and remove any video published from your story.
